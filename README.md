<h1 id="100-awesome-customer-support-tools">100 Awesome Customer Support Tools</h1>


A curated list of 100 customer support tools built to improve customer experience and transform support operations. From AI-powered chatbots and live chat to ticketing systems and helpdesk platforms, this list covers a broad range of solutions for modern, scalable support. The right tools help your team:

- Automate repetitive tasks
- Respond instantly across channels (chat, email, phone, and social channels)
- Deliver AI-first live chat and conversational experiences
- Provide self-service through smart knowledge bases
- Centralize tickets and customer conversations in one place
- Boost overall team productivity
- Lower operational costs while improving customer satisfaction

These 100 trusted tools include everything from CRMs to AI Builders to chatbots, giving you what you need to deliver fast, efficient, and delightful customer support at scale.
